The FIX55 rotu_ai.lua is a behavioral adaptation of MIT-licensed bot
targeting, states, and waypoint/recovery ideas into the original Luanti
VoxeLibre mob API. It does NOT run CoD4 GSC binaries/engine and it does NOT
include CoD4 character models, textures, animations, maps, or audio.
The original NZ:P Viril v26 scores and barricades are independently preserved.
