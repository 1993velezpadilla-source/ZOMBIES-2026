# AK47 source animation coordinate frame and complete-reload framing

The previous pipeline copied native IW3 local XANIM values directly into OAT
glTF nodes. OpenAssetTools v0.34.0 exports global bone transforms as C*G, with
C=Rx(-90 degrees), and exports vertices as (x,z,-y). Child local transforms
remain native, because inverse(C*parent)*(C*child)=inverse(parent)*child.
Root local tracks therefore require C*t and C*q. Applying this conversion to
every child would be incorrect; applying it to no animated root mixes frames.

Primary source: [OAT v0.34.0 GltfWriter.cpp](https://github.com/Laupetin/OpenAssetTools/blob/v0.34.0/src/ObjWriting/XModel/Gltf/GltfWriter.cpp),
LhcToRhcCoordinates, LhcToRhcQuaternion, CreateSkeletonNodes and inverse binds.

This change adapts root tracks in our AK experiment, preserves child tracks,
and renders glTF with Y vertical. A regression creates an independent native
two-joint animated scene, exports OAT-compatible bind frames, evaluates its
glTF skin at five times and compares vertices with C*the native skin result.

Camera candidates now include 38 source poses: idle, three fire times,
17 reload times, and 17 empty-reload times. The score explicitly penalizes
clipping and the worst frame, instead of judging only idle. One fixed camera
is used for all final frames. Complete normal reload is sampled at 24 fps,
empty reload at 12 fps, and GIF centisecond timing matches source duration
within 5 ms. Projection, shading and color calculations are batched; near-plane
crossing triangles retain polygon clipping. Animation channels are cached.

Four local regressions cover native/glTF kinematic equivalence, Y-up camera
and near clipping, mid-reload changes affecting camera selection, and GIF time.
Real-source screenshots and metadata are generated by the private workflow.
Numerical tag alignment and camera framing do not approve finger grip, ADS,
or Android playability. This remains an experiment without a playable AK APK.
